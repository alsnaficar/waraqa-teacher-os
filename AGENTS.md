> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Mobile-First Responsive Design Policy**
>
> - Mobile layout is the highest priority.
> - No horizontal scrolling on phones.
> - No clipped buttons or hidden content.
> - All toolbars must wrap automatically (`flex-wrap`).
> - Cards must stack vertically on small screens.
> - Tables must adapt gracefully for mobile and tablet.
> - Keep touch targets at least 44px (using appropriate `h-11`, `min-h-[44px]`, or paddings).
> - Optimize specifically for Android phones first, then tablets, then desktop.
> - Never sacrifice the mobile experience to improve desktop.
> - Verify pages on Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop before completing tasks.
>   </RULE[AGENTS_md]>
